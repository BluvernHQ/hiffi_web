# Hiffi — ASO Checklist (App Store Optimization)
**Goal:** Appear in App Store + Google Play searches for hip-hop, rap, music video, and independent artist keywords  
**Platforms:** iOS App Store + Google Play Store  

---

## APP NAME

### iOS App Store
- [ ] App name (30 chars max): **"Hiffi: Hip-Hop Music Videos"**
- [ ] Subtitle (30 chars max): **"Stream Rap & Independent Artists"**

### Google Play Store
- [ ] App title (50 chars max): **"Hiffi: Hip-Hop Music & Videos"**
- [ ] Short description (80 chars max): **"Discover independent hip-hop artists. Stream rap music videos. No algorithms."**

> Both stores: primary keyword "hip-hop" must appear in the title or subtitle — it is the single biggest ASO ranking factor.

---

## KEYWORDS (iOS ONLY)

iOS allows 100 characters of hidden keywords. Google Play uses description text instead.

**Suggested keyword field (100 chars):**
```
rap,drill,trap,conscious rap,boom bap,music video,underground rap,hiphop,streaming,independent
```

Rules:
- [ ] No spaces after commas (wastes characters)
- [ ] No words already in app name or subtitle (wasted slots)
- [ ] No competitor names (policy violation)
- [ ] No duplicate keywords
- [ ] Refresh quarterly based on Search Ads data

## DESCRIPTION

**First 255 characters (shown before "More" — most important):**
```
Hiffi is the hip-hop-first music video streaming app for independent rap artists and fans. 
Discover drill, trap, conscious rap, boom bap, and more — no algorithms, no gatekeeping.
```

**Full description structure:**

No algorithm decides who gets seen. No label required to get started. Just the music 
and the fans.

WHAT'S ON HIFFI

Download free. Stream free. Drop your first video today.

### Google Play Store (4000 chars)

Same structure as iOS. Google Play indexes the full description for keyword ranking — repeat key terms naturally:
- "hip-hop" — 5–8 times
- "rap" — 4–6 times
- "music video" — 4–6 times
- "independent artists" — 3–4 times
- Subgenres: drill, trap, conscious rap, boom bap — 2–3 times each

## CATEGORY & METADATA

- [ ] **Primary category:** Music
- [ ] **Secondary category:** Entertainment
- [ ] **Age rating:** 12+ (music with lyrics)
- [ ] **Content rights:** confirm all uploaded content is licensed

### Google Play Store
- [ ] **Category:** Music & Audio
- [ ] **Tags:** add all available: `hip hop`, `rap`, `music video`, `streaming`, `independent music`
- [ ] **Content rating:** Teen (music with lyrics)

## RATINGS & REVIEWS

Ratings directly affect ASO ranking (higher rated apps rank higher).

- [ ] In-app rating prompt — trigger after: user watches 3+ videos, user follows a creator, or user creates a playlist (positive intent moments)
- [ ] Respond to all reviews within 48 hours (Apple and Google both use response rate as a signal)
- [ ] Ask artists / early users for reviews after onboarding

**Target:**
- iOS: 4.5+ stars
- Play: 4.3+ stars

## WHAT TO DO ON LAUNCH / UPDATE

Every app update is an ASO opportunity:

- [ ] Update "What's New" text with release notes that include hip-hop keywords naturally
- [ ] Refresh screenshots if new features are added
- [ ] Monitor keyword rankings weekly for first 4 weeks post-update
- [ ] A/B test icon and screenshots using App Store Product Page Optimization (iOS) or Google Play Experiments (Android)

## APPLE EDITORIAL & GOOGLE FEATURES

Both stores have editorial teams that feature genre-specific apps.

### Apple App Store
- [ ] Submit for consideration: `appstore.apple.com/developer` → App Store Connect → Promote Your App
- [ ] Pitch angle: "First hip-hop-native streaming platform for independent artists"
- [ ] Best timing: around hip-hop cultural moments (BET Awards, Grammy rap nominations, etc.)

### Google Play
- [ ] Apply via Play Console → Store Listing → "Request a feature"
- [ ] Category: Music / Hip-hop
- [ ] Include artist stories / press coverage in pitch
