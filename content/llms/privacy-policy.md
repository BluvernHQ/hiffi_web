## Scope

Applies to all Hiffi users globally. US compliance includes CCPA/CPRA (California), VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA, COPPA, and FTC Act Section 5. International compliance includes India's IT Act 2000, GDPR (EU/UK), and other applicable local laws.

## Information collected

**Audience members:** name, email, phone (if provided), payment info (via third-party processors), viewing/support history, chat interactions, IP address, device identifiers, browser type, usage statistics.

**Artists/creators:** name, email, phone, bank details, government ID (KYC), uploaded content, social handles; SSN/EIN for US artists earning over $600/year.

**Automatic:** cookies, analytics, device info, IP, geolocation, usage data.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information (precise geolocation, biometric ID, etc.) without authorization.

## How information is used

- Operate and maintain the Platform
- Process payments and artist payouts
- Personalize recommendations
- Communications and notices
- Legal/tax/KYC/AML compliance
- Fraud prevention and security
- Analytics and service improvement

## Sharing

Shared with: payment processors, analytics providers (Google Analytics, MsClarity, PostHog), cloud hosts (AWS/GCP), regulators/law enforcement when required, acquirers in mergers.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Analytics sharing may constitute "sharing" under CCPA/CPRA.

## International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country with appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions).

## Cookies

Essential, performance, and analytics cookies. Consent requested for non-essential cookies where required by law.

## Retention

Account data retained during active relationship plus up to 7 years for legal/tax compliance. Deletion requests honored within 30 days except where retention is legally required.

## Your rights

Depending on location: access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object, withdraw consent, data portability.

**California residents:** know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, non-discrimination.

**Exercise rights:** care@hiffi.com (identity verification required; 45-day response, up to 90 with notice).

**Opt out of sale/sharing:** footer link, account settings, email care@hiffi.com, or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.

## Children's privacy (COPPA)

Platform not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. Contact care@hiffi.com if you believe a child provided information.

## Data security

Industry-standard measures including encryption. Breach notification per applicable state laws.
