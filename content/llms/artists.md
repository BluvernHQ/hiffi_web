# Hiffi Artists

> Independent hip-hop artists, rappers, producers, and DJs: create your profile, upload official music videos, and grow your audience on Hiffi.

Hiffi Artists is the creator-facing entry point for independent hip-hop talent. Artists can create a profile, upload official music videos, and reach fans who came for hip-hop — not a generic video feed.
