# Hiffi FAQ

> Frequently asked questions about Hiffi — the hip-hop-first streaming platform for independent rap artists and fans.

Canonical page: https://www.hiffi.com/faq

## Getting Started

### What is Hiffi?

Hiffi is a hip-hop-first music and video streaming platform built for independent rap artists and fans. Hip-hop accounts for roughly 25% of global music streams (IFPI Global Music Report). Hiffi is purpose-built for hip-hop — artists publish music videos and connect directly with audiences, no algorithmic gatekeeping, no label required.

### Where is Hiffi available?

Hiffi is available globally. Artists and listeners from the United States, India, and many other regions can access the platform, subject to local internet regulations.

### How do I create a Hiffi account?

Select Sign Up, add your basic details, verify your email if prompted, and complete your profile to start using the platform.

### Can I reset my password if I forget it?

Yes. Use the Forgot Password page, verify your email with the OTP flow, and set a new password.

### How do I get Hiffi?

Install Hiffi on your phone or tablet from the official download page at https://www.hiffi.com/app (App Store and Google Play links).

## App & downloads

### Is Hiffi available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download from the App Store (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play on supported Android devices. Official store links are on https://www.hiffi.com/app.

### Is Hiffi free to download?

Yes. The Hiffi app is free to download on iOS and Android.

### Is Hiffi for hip-hop and rap artists?

Absolutely. Hiffi is built specifically for the hip-hop and rap community. Independent rappers, drill artists, conscious rap creators, and boom bap producers are welcome. Upload music videos and get discovered without paying for placement. Apply at https://www.hiffi.com/creator/apply.

### What hip-hop subgenres are on Hiffi?

Drill, trap, conscious rap, boom bap, lo-fi hip-hop, melodic rap, rage rap, and more. Browse by mood at https://www.hiffi.com/hip-hop.

### How is Hiffi different from YouTube for rap artists?

Hiffi is purpose-built for hip-hop and rap creators, not a general video platform. Your content reaches an audience that came specifically for hip-hop — no competition with unrelated content, no algorithmic suppression, and creator-first tools.

### Can underground rappers upload to Hiffi?

Yes. Apply through the Become a Creator flow at https://www.hiffi.com/creator/apply — no label, no gatekeeping.

## Watching & Discovery

### How do I find videos on Hiffi?

Browse the home feed at https://www.hiffi.com/ or use Search at https://www.hiffi.com/search.

### What can I do on a video watch page?

Watch the video, open related content, like or dislike, comment, reply, follow the creator, and add videos to playlists.

### Can I like videos and view them later?

Yes. Liked videos are available in the Liked section for signed-in users.

### Can I follow creators?

Yes. Follow or unfollow from profile and watch pages. Following feed shows new uploads from followed creators.

### How do playlists work?

Create playlists, edit names and descriptions, add or remove videos, and play from playlist views.

### Does Hiffi keep watch history?

Yes. Signed-in users can view watch history in the History section.

### Does Hiffi support high-quality video playback?

Yes. Playback quality depends on your device, internet speed, and creator upload settings.

## Creators

### What can creators do on Hiffi?

Set up a profile, upload and manage video content, and build an audience through discoverability and engagement features.

### How do I become a creator on Hiffi?

Use the Become a Creator flow at https://www.hiffi.com/creator/apply. Once approved, creator tools such as upload are available.

### How do creators upload videos?

Use the Upload page (Studio) to submit videos with metadata and thumbnails. Videos are available after processing completes.

### Why does my uploaded video show Processing?

After upload, videos go through processing before playback. While processing, the video card shows a Processing label.

## Account & Support

### Can I edit my profile and profile picture?

Yes. Update profile details and profile picture from your own profile settings.

### How do I report an account or content issue?

While signed in, use Report on a video, comment, or profile. Track cases on My reports. Email care@hiffi.com with case reference, links, and screenshots.

### How do I contact Hiffi support?

Email care@hiffi.com or visit https://www.hiffi.com/support.
