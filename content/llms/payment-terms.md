## 1. Nature of payments

Payments are commercial voluntary support, tips, or subscriptions — not donations or gifts. Hiffi facilitates processing between audience members and artists. Complies with BSA/AML via licensed payment providers. Hiffi is not a bank.

## 2. Payment processing

- Processed via secure third-party gateways (PCI DSS v4.0).
- Hiffi does not store full card details.
- Fraud detection, velocity checks, and risk-based authentication applied.

## 3. Platform fee

A facilitation fee/commission is deducted from each transaction. Fee disclosed at artist onboarding; changes notified 30 days in advance.

## 4. Artist payouts

- Net of platform fee, taxes, chargebacks, and refunds.
- Paid to designated bank account after KYC verification (government ID, SSN/EIN for US artists, bank details).
- Form 1099-K issued for US artists exceeding $600/year.
- International artists: W-8BEN/W-8BEN-E and applicable withholding.

## 5. Payout schedule

Monthly by default, subject to minimum thresholds. Timing depends on processor and bank.

## 6. Taxes

Artists solely responsible for tax obligations in their jurisdiction. Hiffi may withhold where required by law.

## 7. Chargebacks and refunds

- Chargebacks may be deducted from future artist payouts.
- Excessive chargebacks (>1% of transactions) may trigger suspension.
- **General rule:** support payments/tips are non-refundable except where required by law, unauthorized, or benefits not delivered.
- Refund requests: within 7 days via support; response within 5 business days.
- Approved refunds: 7–14 business days to original payment method.

## 8. Fraud prevention / AML

Monitoring per BSA, AML, and OFAC. May hold funds, suspend accounts, or file SARs/CTRs as required. Records retained minimum 5 years.

## 9. Currency

Processed in displayed currency; conversion rates set by processor/bank.

## 10. Liability limit

Payment-related liability capped at lesser of disputed transaction amount or $500 USD.
