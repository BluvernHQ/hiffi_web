# Copyright & DMCA Policy

> How Hiffi handles copyright claims, takedown requests, counter-notices, and repeat infringement.

**Operator:** Kinimi Corporation / Hiffi

## Our commitment

Hiffi operates in compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and applicable intellectual property laws. Creators must upload only content they own or have licensed.

## Reporting copyright infringement

Send a DMCA notice to the Copyright Agent:

- **Email:** care@hiffi.com
- **Subject line:** DMCA Takedown Request

Your notice must include:

1. Physical or electronic signature
2. Identification of the copyrighted work infringed
3. Identification of infringing material on Hiffi (include URLs to video, profile, or other content)
4. Your contact information (address, phone, email)
5. Statement under penalty of perjury of good-faith belief that use is not authorized
6. Statement that information is accurate and you are authorized to act for the copyright owner

## Counter-notices

If content was removed and you believe removal was mistaken or you have authorization, send a counter-notice to care@hiffi.com including:

1. Your signature
2. Identification of removed content and its prior location
3. Good-faith statement that removal was by mistake or misidentification
4. Your name, address, and telephone number
5. Consent to U.S. District Court jurisdiction

Content may be restored within 10–14 business days unless legal action is filed.

## Repeat infringers

Accounts receiving multiple valid DMCA takedown notices may be suspended or permanently terminated.
