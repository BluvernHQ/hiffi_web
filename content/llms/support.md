# Hiffi Support

> Help with your account, payments, streams, content reports, and feedback.

Canonical page: https://www.hiffi.com/support

## How we can help

Hiffi is a global platform that helps artists stream live performances, share recorded content, and receive support from audiences through voluntary payments, tips, and subscriptions.

For account issues, payments, streams, feedback, or feature requests, contact **care@hiffi.com**.

## Content reports

Use the Report option on videos, comments, or profiles while signed in. Track submitted cases at https://www.hiffi.com/support/reports.

## Contact options

- **Email support:** care@hiffi.com — general questions, account issues, problem reports
- **FAQ:** https://www.hiffi.com/faq — common questions about accounts, discovery, and creators
