# How Hiffi works

> Whether you're discovering new rap or sharing your own music videos, Hiffi is built to be straightforward — sign up, explore hip-hop culture, and connect without gatekeepers.

Canonical page: https://www.hiffi.com/how-it-works

## For fans

1. **Sign up free** — Create an account at https://www.hiffi.com/signup or download the mobile app at https://www.hiffi.com/app.
2. **Discover** — Browse the home feed, explore the hip-hop hub (https://www.hiffi.com/hip-hop), or use Search (https://www.hiffi.com/search).
3. **Engage** — Follow creators, like videos, comment, save favorites, and build playlists.
4. **Stay connected** — Use Following to see new uploads from artists you care about.

## For creators

1. **Become a creator** — Hip-hop and rap artists unlock creator access instantly at https://www.hiffi.com/creator/apply.
2. **Set up your profile** — Add your bio, profile picture, and links.
3. **Upload** — Publish official music videos through Studio (https://www.hiffi.com/studio).
4. **Grow** — Get discovered via the hip-hop hub, mood playlists, search, and the home feed.

## Mood-based discovery

Hiffi organizes hip-hop by native moods (On Sight, Soul Search, Money Talk, Blue Hours, Low Rider, Turn Up, God's Plan). Browse at https://www.hiffi.com/hip-hop.
