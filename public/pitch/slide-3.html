THE PROBLEM

The internet created infinite music. It did not create
better discovery.

Three parts of the ecosystem experience the same missing signal in different ways.

FOR ARTISTS

  • Hard to break through
  • No real view of audience sentiment
  • Opportunities arrive too late

FOR FANS

  • Too much content
  • Emerging talent is hard to find
  • Algorithms follow popularity

FOR INDUSTRY

  • Weak early signals
  • Scouting is expensive
  • Decision-making is slow
DISCOVERY
GAP

Same ecosystem. Different symptoms. One missing intelligence layer.
