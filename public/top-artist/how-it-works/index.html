Method / How it works

Built on signal.
Not opinion.

The Hiffi Hip-Hop 500 ranks artists by current YouTube popularity and momentum — scale, velocity, and growth — not artistic greatness. Rank is never for sale.

Enter live ranking → Claim your profile ↗

01 / Pipeline

From channel
to chart.

  1. 01

    Inventory + YouTube

    Artists in the Hiffi inventory with a YouTube channel are scored by the ranking worker. Channels without metrics stay off the chart until a successful refresh.

  2. 02

    Global Top 500

    The live leaderboard is the global YouTube composite ranking — up to 500 artists ordered by score. Filter chips load cities with enough ranked artists, then open that city’s local top 50.

  3. 03

    City charts

    City banners use exact location strings (for example Atlanta, GA). City ranks are local 1–50 by composite score — not a substring cut of the global list.

  4. 04

    Claim & verify

    Artists can claim their Artist Index profile to verify links, upload videos, and get discovered — separate from the ranking score itself.

Modes / Preview

Risers, breakout,
heat, and verified.

Overall is the full live chart. Biggest Risers uses weekly rank snapshots to surface the largest climbs. Underground Heat is a dedicated mid/small-reach board sorted by engagement plus momentum. Breakout 100 is reach-below-P70 artists sorted by momentum. Verified ranking filters the chart payload to claimed Artist Index profiles.
