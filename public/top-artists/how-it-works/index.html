Method / How it works

Built on signal.
Not opinion.

The Hiffi Hip-Hop 500 ranks artists by a blended YouTube + Instagram score — scale, velocity, and growth — not artistic greatness. Rank is never for sale.

Enter live ranking → Claim your profile ↗

01 / Pipeline

From channel
to chart.

  1. 01

    Inventory + platforms

    Artists in the Hiffi inventory are scored from YouTube and Instagram. Overall and city ranks use a blended final score across both platforms.

  2. 02

    Global Top 500

    The live leaderboard is the global blended ranking — up to 500 artists ordered by final score. Filter chips load cities with enough ranked artists, then open that city’s local top 50.

  3. 03

    City charts

    City banners use exact location strings (for example Atlanta, GA). City ranks are local 1–50 by composite score — not a substring cut of the global list.

  4. 04

    Claim & verify

    Artists can claim their Artist Index profile to verify links, upload videos, and get discovered — separate from the ranking score itself.

Modes / Preview

Risers, breakout,
and underground heat.

Overall is the full live chart. Biggest Risers uses weekly rank snapshots to surface the largest climbs. Underground Heat is a dedicated mid/small-reach board sorted by engagement plus momentum. Breakout 100 is reach-below-P70 artists sorted by momentum.

Note / v1

YouTube + Instagram
blend.

Overall and city charts use a blended final score from YouTube and Instagram. Platform reach fields fill in as scrape history grows; momentum reads as zero until those windows exist. Risers, Breakout, and Underground Heat remain mode-specific boards and do not use the Instagram blend.
